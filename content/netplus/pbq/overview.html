What a PBQ is

  • A task instead of four answer choices. You type into a switch console, drag cables onto a diagram, fill in a firewall table, or assign subnets to a router.
  • Several fields per question. Each field is its own decision, so treat every one as if it is scored separately. Never leave one blank.
  • They usually come first in the exam. You can flag one, move on, and come back.
Time budget
  • 90 minutes for up to 90 questions. One PBQ can take 8 to 10 minutes.
  • Fill in what you are sure of. If the rest isn't coming after about 5 minutes, flag it and come back after the multiple-choice questions.

Five steps for any PBQ

  1. Read every requirement first. Phrases like "smallest subnet" or "only the admin workstation" each decide one field.
  2. Count what you have. More drag items than slots means some are distractors.
  3. Gather evidence before answering. In a console, run every command on every device.
  4. Answer the sure fields first. A known subnet gives you the gateway. A known VLAN gives you the trunk.
  5. Re-check every requirement against your answers before moving on.
