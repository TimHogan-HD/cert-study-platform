PBQ Lab

PBQ Exam Mode

A timed set of random PBQs with no checking until you submit. The closest thing here to the start of the real exam.

  • Each PBQ comes from a different lab. Addressing, routing, and firewall PBQs get fresh numbers every time.
  • One clock for the whole set. It adds up each PBQ's exam pace, and the exam submits itself when time runs out.
  • No Check or Show answers buttons. Move between PBQs with the numbers or Previous and Next, and flag any you want to come back to.
  • Submit to see your score, then review every PBQ with its wrong fields marked and its explanation.
