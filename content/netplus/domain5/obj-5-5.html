5.5 Tools & Protocols

Official objective 5.5 is "Given a scenario, use the appropriate tool or protocol to solve networking issues." Most of the underlying material is already covered in 5.2 and 5.3 — this section indexes it by tool category.

Where the Tool Coverage Lives
  • 5.2 Cable & Physical Issues — physical tools: cable tester, TDR, OTDR, toner probe (fox and hound), visual fault locator, multimeter, loopback plug.
  • 5.3 Network Issues — command-line tools: ping, traceroute, nslookup, netstat, arp, route, nmap, pathping, iperf3, tcpdump, Wireshark, and the Cisco show commands.

Work through both sections when preparing for objective 5.5.
