Objective 1.4

Ports & Protocols

Memorize the port table. You will see port number questions on the exam. Focus especially on the ones with non-obvious transports or common confusions.

TCP vs UDP

FeatureTCPUDP
ConnectionConnection-oriented (3-way handshake: SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK)Connectionless — fire and forget
ReliabilityGuaranteed delivery, retransmission of lost packetsNo guarantee; best-effort
OrderingSequenced — packets arrive in orderNo sequencing
SpeedSlower (overhead for acknowledgment)Faster (no overhead)
Use CasesHTTP, HTTPS, FTP, SSH, SMTP, emailDNS queries, DHCP, SNMP, VoIP, streaming, TFTP

⚠ Exam Traps
  • DNS uses both UDP and TCP — don't say "DNS is always UDP." Zone transfers use TCP.
  • RADIUS uses UDP. TACACS+ uses TCP. This is the #1 RADIUS/TACACS+ mistake.
  • SNMP trap direction: agent sends trap TO manager on port 162. Manager polls agent on 161.
  • FTP has TWO ports: 20 (data) and 21 (control). Many questions test whether you know both.
  • IMAP (143) keeps mail on server. POP3 (110) downloads and deletes — important for multi-device scenarios.
Quick Recall — 1.4
  • SSH=22 | Telnet=23 | SMTP=25 | DNS=53 | DHCP=67/68 | HTTP=80 | HTTPS=443
  • SNMP: 161 (poll) / 162 (trap) — both UDP
  • RADIUS: 1812/1813 UDP | TACACS+: 49 TCP (full encryption)
  • RDP=3389 TCP | LDAP=389 / LDAPS=636 TCP | NTP=123 UDP | Syslog=514 UDP
  • TCP = reliable, connection-oriented | UDP = fast, connectionless
