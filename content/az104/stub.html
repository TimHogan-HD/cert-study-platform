AZ-104: Azure Administrator

AZ-104 validates skills for implementing, managing, and monitoring an organization's Azure environment. It's the core Azure administrator certification and a prerequisite for many Azure specialty certs.

Recommended Study Path

AZ-900 (Azure Fundamentals) is strongly recommended before AZ-104. If you completed the AZ-900 cram app, you already have the foundational cloud concepts. AZ-104 dives into hands-on administration — plan time in the Azure portal with a free account alongside your reading.
